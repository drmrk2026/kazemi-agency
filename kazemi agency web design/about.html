Background

I design and build the technical side of Kazemi AI Agency myself — frontend, scraping tools, and the servers that keep it all running. Before starting the agency, I worked directly with businesses that needed a fast, dependable corporate web presence and no patience for months-long agency timelines. That's the gap the three fixed packages on this site are built to close.

Outside of client work, I build internal tools: an n8n-based pipeline for keeping WordPress content current, and IR-Cyber Scraper, a branded Google Maps lead-generation tool used inside the agency's own workflow.
